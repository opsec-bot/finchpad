## Long-form

# Finchpad is live on Robinhood Chain

Finchpad is a token launchpad where the anti-rug guarantees are in the contracts, not in a
roadmap. Launch a token in one transaction — fixed 1e9 supply, Uniswap V3 pool created and
liquidity locked in the same block. No bonding curve, no migration event, no moment where
the deployer holds the LP.

**Creators keep 80% of trading fees.** The pool takes the standard 1% fee; finchpad's
protocol cut is 20% of that — deliberately more generous than the 70/30 you get elsewhere.
Flat launch fee: 0.0005 ETH.

**And the split gets better as your token proves itself.** Graduation is measured in fees
your token has actually earned — not a pool balance someone can donate into. Cross the
threshold and the protocol cut drops from 20% to 15%, with the difference going to you.
It's derived from the locker's own accounting, so it can't be faked or gamed.

**Referrals pay real money.** Name a referrer at launch and they earn 10% of the protocol's
cut on every trade of that token, forever — funded entirely from our side. The creator's
80% is never touched.

**Anti-rug by construction, not by promise.** Fixed 1e9 supply, no transfer hooks, no
launch windows — a token that can't restrict transfers is a token you don't have to trust.
The LP position is locked in the locker permanently; there is no migration event where
liquidity changes hands. Fee splits are snapshotted at launch and immutable. Creators can
lock their own allocation with on-chain vesting (cliff/linear) as a verifiable "I can't
dump."

**And the rest of the toolbox:** paid featured placement for visibility, community
takeover paths for abandoned tokens, and GitHub-bound launches (bind a token's fee rights
to a repo or user, with fees escrowed until the owner claims). All optional, all on-chain.

**Everything is inspectable.**

- Contracts verified on the Robinhood Chain explorer: [EXPLORER LINKS]
- Internal security reviews are published in the repo. We have **not yet** had a
  third-party audit — we're a small team being straight with you about that. There's a
  standing bug bounty (see SECURITY.md), and a professional audit is committed once
  protocol revenue funds it.
- Fee splits are snapshotted per token at launch and immutable afterward. The protocol fee
  recipient is immutable in the factory — we couldn't redirect it if we wanted to.
